Submarine Fishing Task

Try a short demo of the study

On each turn, three sea creatures flash briefly on the screen, and you click where you remember each one appearing. Then you choose which creature to catch. The bars show how much of each is available: green adds to your net, red takes away from it. A net can only hold one type of creature, so if you switch to a different one, the net empties and starts over.

This demo ends when you fill one net or run out of turns. No data is recorded.
