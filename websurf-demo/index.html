Web-Surf Task

Try a short demo of the study

You will browse a few video galleries. Each gallery offers a video and tells you how long it will take to load. You can load the video and wait, or skip to the next gallery. Sometimes the wait gets longer after you have started, and you can stop waiting at any time.

This demo has 4 offers, one from each gallery, with shorter waits than the full study. No data is recorded.
