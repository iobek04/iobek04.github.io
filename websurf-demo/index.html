Interactive demo — a short taste of the real study

You'll browse a few video galleries. Before each video plays, you're told how long it will take to load. You decide: is it worth the wait, or do you skip to a different gallery? Sometimes the wait gets longer once you've already started — do you keep waiting, or cut your losses?

This demo runs 3 quick rounds. No data is recorded.
