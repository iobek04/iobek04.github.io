Outside the Lab

Young Guru Academy

Alumni Mentor, 2019–present

Since 2019, I have mentored Turkish high school and university students pursuing international education and STEM careers through Young Guru Academy (YGA). I also led STEM outreach across more than five cities in rural Anatolia, training teachers and students and bringing hands-on science education to underserved communities.

I shared this work in a talk at YGA's annual summit, which reaches around 20,000 attendees.
